Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 21, 2026

Our Privacy Pledge

At Specline Tech LLC, we believe your music taste is your business. Social Jukebox is designed to be Privacy-First. We do not create user accounts, we do not track your location, and we do not sell your data. Period.

Data Collection

What we DON'T collect:

  • Names or Emails (for app accounts)
  • Phone Numbers
  • Precise GPS Location
  • Contacts or Photos
  • Listening history or music libraries on our servers

What happens locally:

  • Apple Music authentication
  • Spotify authentication (via Spotify app)
  • Device-to-device voting over your local network
  • Temporary session IDs on your devices

Local Sessions vs. Our Website

Party sessions (queues, votes, and guest connections) run on your devices over the local network. They are not stored on Specline Tech LLC servers.

Separately, this website and a small Spotify token proxy are hosted on our infrastructure so the app can keep Spotify’s client secret off your device and so we can publish support and legal pages.

Third Party Services

Social Jukebox and this website use the following third parties:

  • Apple Music: Playback uses Apple’s MusicKit framework. Your interaction with Apple Music content is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not transmit your music library data to any Specline Tech LLC servers.
  • Spotify: Playback uses the Spotify iOS SDK. Authorization tokens are exchanged through a Specline Tech LLC proxy on socialjukebox.app solely to keep the Spotify client secret off your device: we do not store listening history, account data, or personal information from that exchange. Your use of Spotify is also governed by Spotify’s Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel Analytics & Speed Insights: Our website uses Vercel’s privacy-oriented analytics to understand aggregate traffic and performance, including optional custom events such as App Store button clicks (page path and button placement only. No personal identifiers). These tools are designed to avoid invasive cross-site tracking; see Vercel’s documentation for details.
  • Chatbase: Our support pages may load a Chatbase assistant widget to answer product questions. Messages you send in that chat are processed by Chatbase under their privacy policy so we can provide support.
