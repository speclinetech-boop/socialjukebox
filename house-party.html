"It started with a problem everyone knows: the Playlist Dictator."

Social Jukebox came from nights where one person was stuck on the aux while everyone else argued about what should play next. Music at a house party should be a conversation, not a monologue.

So we built a democratic DJ for personal hangouts: living rooms, backyards, birthday bashes with friends. Guests join nearby, vote on the queue, and the crowd decides. No bars. No venues. Just your people and better music nights.
