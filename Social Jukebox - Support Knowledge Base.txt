What is Social Jukebox?

Social Jukebox is a democratic DJ app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. One person acts as the DJ (host) and creates a party session with a 4-digit PIN. Friends join as guests (with a name). Everyone can add songs and vote on what plays next: songs with the most votes rise to the top automatically.
• Works with Apple Music AND Spotify. The DJ picks the service
• Guests do NOT need an Apple Music or Spotify account
• All devices must be on the same WiFi network (or nearby via Bluetooth)
• No accounts needed to join. Just a name and a 4-digit PIN
• Available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Apple Silicon)
• Android is not yet supported

Music Services. Apple Music & Spotify

Social Jukebox supports two music services. The DJ picks one when starting a session. Guests interact with the queue the same way regardless of which service the DJ chose.

Feature comparison:
• DJ subscription required. Apple Music: Apple Music subscription | Spotify: Spotify app + account
• Guest subscription required. Apple Music: No | Spotify: No
• Playlist import. Apple Music: Yes | Spotify: Yes
• Full catalog search. Apple Music: Yes | Spotify: Yes
• Brief app handoff on first use. Apple Music: No | Spotify: Yes (normal behavior)

Apple Music
• Requires an active Apple Music subscription on the DJ's device
• Full catalog access via Apple's MusicKit framework
• Import playlists directly from the DJ's Apple Music library
• Guests do NOT need an Apple Music subscription

Spotify
• Requires the Spotify app installed on the DJ's device
• Full catalog access and playlist import from the DJ's Spotify library
• When Spotify is selected for the first time, the app briefly opens the Spotify app to authorize: you'll see an "Opening Spotify..." message and return automatically
• Returning DJs with a saved Spotify login skip this step entirely
• Guests do NOT need a Spotify account

Getting Started

How to Start a Party (DJ)
1. Open Social Jukebox and tap "I'm the DJ"
2. Pick your music service: Apple Music or Spotify
3. If using Spotify for the first time, the app briefly opens Spotify to authorize. This is normal
4. A 4-digit PIN appears: share it with your guests
5. Import a playlist or search for songs to build your queue
6. Tap "Play Next Song" to start the music

Hosting requires DJ Pro ($1.99 one-time purchase). Joining as a guest is always free.

How to Join a Party (Guest)
1. Open Social Jukebox and tap "I'm a Guest"
2. Enter your display name (required in 3.0)
3. Enter the 4-digit PIN from the DJ
4. Tap "Join Party"
5. You're connected! Add songs, vote, and send reactions

Make sure you're on the same WiFi network as the DJ.

Adding Music

How to Add Songs
• Tap "Add Song or Playlist"
• Search by title or artist
• Tap the "+" button next to any song
• The song appears in the queue for everyone to vote on
• Everyone can see who added each song

How to Import a Playlist
• Tap "Add Song or Playlist" then "Import from Playlist"
• Select a playlist from your Apple Music or Spotify library
• Songs are added to the party queue

Limits: Guests can import up to 50 songs per playlist. DJs with DJ Pro can import unlimited songs.
Spotify DJs: Playlist access is available immediately after picking Spotify: you don't need to play a song first.

Voting System

How Voting Works
• Upvote (up arrow): Moves the song higher in the queue
• Downvote (down arrow): Moves the song lower in the queue
• Songs with the most votes play first
• You can change your vote at any time

What are Super Votes?
Super Votes count for significantly more than a standard vote, pushing a song toward the top instantly.
• Everyone gets 3 free Super Votes per session
• Look for the lightning bolt icon next to songs to use a Super Vote
• Remaining Super Votes are shown in the top-right corner

Can I Remove a Song?
Individual songs cannot be removed. To clear the queue, the DJ can end the session and start a new one. Alternatively, downvote songs you don't want: they'll sink to the bottom. A song can also be auto-skipped or removed when a majority of voters have downvoted it (with at least 3 people voting) AND its overall score drops below zero. Super Votes help protect a song because their boost must be overcome before the score can go negative. The DJ's "play now" / Force Play pick is never auto-skipped.

DJ Controls

What Controls Does the DJ Have?
• Play/Pause: Control when music plays
• Play Next Song: Advance to the next song in the queue
• Force Play: Tap any song to play it immediately, bypassing vote order
• Invite: Share the party PIN with friends
• Guest list: See who's connected by name
• Kick guest: Remove someone who's wrecking the vibe
• Participant count: Shows how many guests are connected

Only the DJ can skip songs, kick guests, or control playback. Guests cannot skip. They influence the queue through votes.

Emoji Reactions

Tap any emoji at the bottom of the screen. It floats across everyone's screen in real-time. Tap the smiley face button to see more options.
Quick reactions: fire, heart, cool face, party popper, music note, wine glass, dancer, bus

Session & Connection

What Happens if the DJ Closes the App?
With DJ Pro:
• Session is automatically saved
• Reopen the app and tap "Resume Session"
• Queue and current song are restored; guests rejoin with the same PIN
Without DJ Pro:
• Session ends when the app is closed
• All queue data is lost

Why Can't I Connect?
• Same WiFi network: You must be on the exact same WiFi as the DJ
• Correct PIN: Double-check the 4-digit code
• Local Network permission: On iPhone/iPad, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure Social Jukebox is on
• DJ is still hosting: Make sure the DJ hasn't ended the session or kicked you
• Wait a moment: Connection sometimes takes a few seconds

Background Playback & CarPlay
• Music continues playing when the DJ switches apps or locks the phone
• Lock screen controls work (play, pause, skip)
• CarPlay is fully supported: music plays through car speakers with basic controls on the CarPlay screen
• Guests can switch apps and rejoin; the party continues without them

Privacy & Data
Social Jukebox does not collect personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising. All party data stays on local devices. Communication happens peer-to-peer between devices. Guest display names are shared only with other devices in the party session.
Apple Music: Only searches the catalog and plays queued songs. Your library stays private.
Spotify: Authorization tokens are exchanged through a secure proxy server (socialjukebox.app) to protect your credentials. We do not store Spotify account data or listening history.

Troubleshooting

Spotify-Specific Issues
• "Opening Spotify..." appears briefly. Normal on first use. The app authorizes via the Spotify app and returns automatically.
• Songs getting cut off at the end. Update to version 3.0 or later. Fixed in 2.2+.
• Timer stuck at 0:00 during playback. Update to version 3.0 or later. Fixed in 2.2+.
• Playlists not loading before first play. Update to version 3.0 or later. Playlists load immediately after picking Spotify.

Apple Music-Specific Issues
• Resume Last Session freezes. Update to version 3.0 or later. Fixed in 2.2+.
• Songs not advancing automatically. Update to version 3.0 or later. Playback transitions improved.

General Issues
• Music won't play: Check subscription is active / Spotify app is installed. Check volume and Bluetooth.
• App running slowly: Close background apps, ensure strong WiFi, wait for large playlist imports to finish.
• Songs not syncing: Confirm all devices on same WiFi, check PIN matches, have guest leave and rejoin.
• Can't hear music as a guest: Only the DJ's device plays audio. This is by design.
• Can't find a song: Check spelling, try artist name instead of song title, some tracks vary by region.
• Got kicked from the party: The DJ removed you. Ask them for the PIN again if they want you back: you'll need to rejoin with your name.

Tips for a Great Party

For DJs
• Pre-load a playlist before guests arrive
• Pick your music service before guests arrive. Spotify auth only takes a moment
• Share the PIN using the green Invite button
• Use Force Play when the moment calls for a specific song
• Use the guest list and kick when someone is wrecking the vibe
• Keep your phone plugged in. DJ mode uses battery
For Guests
• Pick a clear display name so people know who added what
• Use Super Votes wisely: you only get 3 per session
• Import a playlist instead of adding songs one by one
• Vote on existing songs to influence what plays next
• Send reactions to hype up good songs
