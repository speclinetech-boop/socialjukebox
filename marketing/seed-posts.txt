1) ROAD TRIP — Reddit / FB travel groups / Discord
────────────────────────────────────────
Subject/title: End aux fights on road trips — everyone votes on the playlist

Body:
Shotgun used to mean “you get the aux.” Now it just means arguments.

Social Jukebox lets one person host Apple Music or Spotify, share a 4-digit PIN, and everyone in the car votes on what plays next. Guests don’t need a music subscription.

https://socialjukebox.app/road-trip

(iPhone / iPad / Mac — free to join, $1.99 one-time to host)

2) HOUSE PARTY — party hosting / college / apartment groups
────────────────────────────────────────
Subject/title: Stop hogging the aux at house parties

Body:
If one person always DJs, half the room is annoyed by song 3.

Social Jukebox is a democratic DJ: host starts a session, guests join with a PIN, upvote/downvote the queue. Works with Apple Music and Spotify. Guests don’t need accounts.

https://socialjukebox.app/house-party
