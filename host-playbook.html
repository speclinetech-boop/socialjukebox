5-minute host setup

Do this before the first guest asks for the aux.

  1. Open Social Jukebox → I'm the DJ

    Hosting needs DJ Pro (one-time unlock). Guests join free — they never pay to vote.

  2. Pick Apple Music or Spotify — and approve every popup

    iOS and the music apps will ask for permission. Tap Allow / authorize when prompted. Spotify may briefly open Spotify to authorize playback — that's normal. Guests don't need that account; they just join and vote.

  3. Seed 5–10 songs so the queue isn't empty

    A blank queue is awkward. Drop a short opener list, then let the room take over.

  4. Share the 4-digit PIN — and warn guests about the Local Network popup

    Guests tap I'm a Guest and enter the PIN. Same Wi‑Fi as you. When iOS asks for Local Network access, they must tap Allow — if they miss or deny it, they won't find the party.

  5. Say the one-liner rule once

    “Add what you want. Upvote the bangers. Downvote the duds. The queue decides — not the person with the cord.”

Don't miss the popups

People skip these in a hurry — then blame the app when they can't join. If a system popup appears, tap Allow. Denying Local Network is the #1 reason guests never find the party.

1 Local Network (DJ + every guest)

iOS will ask something like: “Social Jukebox would like to find and connect to devices on your local network.”

→ Tap Allow. Every time. Host and guests.

If someone already tapped Don't Allow (or the popup never showed): Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → turn on Social Jukebox. If it's missing from the list, open the app and try joining again so iOS re-prompts.

2 Music / Spotify authorization (DJ only)

When you pick a music service, Apple Music or Spotify may ask for access. Spotify may briefly jump into the Spotify app to authorize playback — that's expected.

→ Approve it. Guests never need this step.

3 Same Wi‑Fi (everyone)

Allowing Local Network only works if you're on the exact same Wi‑Fi as the DJ — not cellular, not a hotel network that isolates devices, not “same building, different band names.”

Host tip: before you share the PIN, say — “When your phone asks about Local Network, tap Allow or you won't see the party.”

House rules that stop aux fights

Post these in the group chat. Takes 10 seconds. Saves the night.

No aux monopoly

Nobody sits on the cable all night. Everyone adds from their own phone. Votes move the queue.

Votes > opinions shouted across the room

Hate a track? Downvote it. Love it? Upvote. Debating “who put this on” out loud is optional theater.

Super Votes are rare — treat them that way

Everyone gets 3 free Super Votes per session (+5 and a shield). Save them for the song you'll regret missing — not every opener.

Own your add

Every song shows who added it. Celebrate the banger. Gently roast the dud. That's the fun — not a fight over the cord.

DJ keeps the nuclear option

You can still skip, play now, or kick a guest if someone is wrecking the vibe. Democracy with a host — not chaos.

Pro tips from real hosts

The stuff that usually breaks a session — solved early.

Guest stuck on a spinner? They denied Local Network

iOS fails silently if Local Network is off — no useful error, just “can't find the party.” Fix: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Social Jukebox ON. Then re-enter the PIN. See Don't miss the popups.

Mac near the speakers = DJ dashboard

For house parties, run the host on a Mac by the speaker. Guests vote from phones. You see the guest list, who added what, and the live queue on a big screen.

Names on join keep the room honest

Guests enter a name before they join. You see who's here. No more anonymous queue bombers.
