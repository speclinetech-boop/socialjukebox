How it works for road trips

Three steps. No cables. Every track shows who added it.

1

Host from the driver's seat

2

Passengers join with a PIN

Share the 4-digit PIN. Back seat, shotgun, the kid with AirPods: everyone joins from their own phone. No accounts required for guests.

Add songs, upvote the bangers, downvote the skips. The queue reorders itself so the whole car shapes the soundtrack.

"It started with a problem everyone knows: the Playlist Dictator."

Every road trip has the same soundtrack fight: shotgun grabs the aux, the back seat mutinies, and somehow you're still stuck on the same three songs. Handing over an unlocked phone doesn't fix it. It just starts a new argument.

So we built a democratic queue for the car. Host from your phone. Now Playing and the party PIN show on Apple CarPlay. Passengers join with a PIN. Votes decide what plays next. Music becomes a conversation, not a monologue. Whether you're crossing state lines or just driving across town.
