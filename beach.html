How it works on beach day

Three steps. No aux cord. No playlist dictator.

1

You host

Open Social Jukebox, connect Apple Music or Spotify, and start the beach session from your phone.

2

Friends join with a PIN

Share the 4-digit code. Everyone on the towels joins in seconds — no accounts required for guests.

3

Vote. Let the best song win.

Add tracks, upvote the bangers, downvote the duds. The queue reorders itself — the crowd decides.

Why this beats shared aux

One person with the cord is a monologue. A beach day deserves a conversation.

No more hogging the aux

Nobody sits on the speaker all afternoon. Everyone adds songs from their own phone — sand, sunscreen, and all.

Votes settle the fight

Skip the “whose turn is it?” debate. Upvotes move the hits up; the playlist dictator finally clocks out.

Built for the crew, not a venue

Personal and social only — friends, family, and the group you came with. Not for bars or commercial use.

Born on the Beach

Aruba, 2025

"It started with a problem everyone knows: the Playlist Dictator."

Social Jukebox was born on a beach in Aruba, surrounded by friends and family. One person struggled to keep the music going while trying to please everyone. Every song change brought eye rolls — or someone asking for the aux.

That's when it clicked: music at a gathering should be a conversation, not a monologue. So we built a democratic DJ for the sand — the same idea that works for road trips and house parties, too.

Family-tested, beach-approved, and ready to end the era of the Playlist Dictator.
